# Product Vision: Spectral Notes
Spectral Notes is a minimalist, spooky note-taking application designed for the Kiroween 2026 Costume Contest. 

## Core Concept
It solves the problem of task procrastination by gamifying the guilt of unfinished tasks. Notes that are left incomplete for over 24 hours become "Haunted" (displaying a glowing ghost icon and a flicker animation). Completing the note "exorcises" it.

## Design Language
- Aesthetic: Haunted house meets sleek tech.
- Colors: Deep blacks, blood reds, and ghostly whites.
- Typography: Clean sans-serif for reading, spooky display font for headings.
- Motion: Everything should feel slightly ethereal (floating, fading, flickering).
